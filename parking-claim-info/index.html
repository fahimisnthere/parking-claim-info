Parking Claim Automation

A personal tool maintained by Fahim to prepare parking claims from Touch ’n Go card transaction statements.

What the app does

  1. Reads matching statement emails and PDF attachments from the owner's connected Gmail account.
  2. Finds Tamarind Square parking transactions and calculates the claim amount.
  3. Prepares a statement copy with unrelated transaction rows visually covered.
  4. Saves statement files to the owner's Google Drive and writes the date, description and total to a designated Google Sheets claim form.

The monthly claim tab follows the statement end date. The total covers matching transactions across the entire statement period.

This is a personal-use application, not a public registration service. It does not request statements from Touch ’n Go or submit claims to an employer.

Google account access

The app uses Google authorization to read Gmail, edit the designated claim spreadsheet, and manage files it creates or is granted access to in Drive. The owner can revoke access at any time.
